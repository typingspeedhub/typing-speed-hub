Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Who operates this website

Data controller: Tibor Győry, operator of Typing Speed Hub, Hungary.

For privacy questions and requests, email contact@typingspeedhub.com. See also our About page and Impressum.

This policy describes the site's practice tools, local browser storage, optional Google Analytics, hosting, and contact communications. You can use the typing tools without accepting optional analytics. No account is required.

2. Your privacy choice

Use the Privacy settings button at the bottom-left of every content page to accept or reject Google Analytics or change your choice. Optional analytics is off until you explicitly opt in. Closing the panel without saving does not grant permission.

Your choice is stored in your browser under tsh_privacy_choice_v1 for up to 180 days. After that, or after you clear site data, we ask again. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice may apply only to the current page.

Withdrawal stops future analytics collection from this site; it does not automatically delete information already processed by Google. Contact us about applicable deletion rights.

3. Practice data stored on your device

The typing tests, lessons, and progress tools process the text you type and calculate results in your browser. Depending on the tool, browser localStorage remembers WPM, accuracy, elapsed time, session dates, personal bests, exercise history, or unlocked lessons.

Storage keys include typingSpeedHubPracticeStatsV1, tsh_touch_typing_unlock_v2, and tsh_numbers_symbols_unlock_v1. Other tools may use their own local keys for results or settings. This data stays on the current browser and device and does not automatically synchronize across devices.

The site does not upload your practice text or saved results to a Typing Speed Hub server. We do not deliberately send them as Google Analytics events. CSV exports are downloaded to your device; we do not receive those files unless you separately send them to us.

Local results and lesson progress remain until you use an available reset/clear control or delete this site's browser data. Rejecting analytics does not erase your practice records.

4. Optional Google Analytics

Some content and tool pages use Google Analytics 4, measurement ID G-68ZVCW4FWK, only after you accept analytics. Pages without Analytics, including the dedicated Typing for Kids page, still let you manage the site-wide choice.

After acceptance, Google may receive page addresses, referrer information, visit and interaction events, browser/device information, cookie identifiers, and approximate location. Network communication also necessarily exposes technical connection information to the receiving service. Avoid putting personal information into a page URL.

We use visit statistics to understand which pages are useful and improve the site. Google Analytics is not loaded on a fresh visit before consent or after rejection. This implementation disables Google signals and advertising-personalization signals in the Analytics configuration and keeps advertising consent denied.

The legal basis for optional Analytics is consent. Cookies created by this implementation are configured with a maximum lifetime of 180 days and without automatic activity-based renewal; the browser or Google may shorten their lifetime. Withdrawal attempts to remove this site's accessible Analytics cookies and reloads a page if Analytics was already running.

Google's service-side user/event retention is controlled separately in the Analytics property; deleting browser cookies does not itself delete Google's stored data. Contact us for information about the current property setting and retention applicable to a request.

Provider information: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and Analytics retention controls.

5. Hosting and essential technical processing

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub documents that visitors' IP addresses are logged and stored for security purposes, even when a visitor is not signed in. Technical delivery and security processing is separate from optional Analytics and is not disabled by rejecting analytics.

Hosting provider: GitHub, Inc. See GitHub Pages documentation and the GitHub Privacy Statement for hosting-related processing and retention information.

We rely on legitimate interests in delivering and securing the website where applicable. Local progress storage provides the practice and tracking features you choose to use. The privacy-choice record remembers and applies your selection.

6. Advertising status

This version of the website does not load advertising scripts or request Google ads. An AdSense account-verification meta tag and the public ads.txt file identify the publisher; neither is an advertising script.

The site's analytics choice panel is not a Google-certified advertising CMP and does not collect IAB TCF advertising consent. Before advertising is enabled, the necessary advertising-consent solution and disclosures must be configured. This policy will be updated to describe the actual advertising partners and processing when implemented.

7. External and affiliate links

The archived homepage loads Font Awesome styling from the cdnjs content delivery network. Loading that resource sends technical request information, including your IP address, to the CDN provider. The Analytics choice does not control that styling resource. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Our pages link to external tools, products, social networks, and educational resources. A destination website may process your information under its own privacy policy when you follow a link. Where a link is an affiliate link, the relevant page should disclose that relationship; a purchase may result in a commission at no additional cost to you.

This package does not load an affiliate tracking script into our pages. Affiliate destinations may still use referral information or tracking when visited. We do not receive your payment details from ordinary outbound links.

8. Contact information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any name or attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your inquiry and keep relevant correspondence. Please do not send passwords, financial account information, or unnecessary sensitive information.

We rely on legitimate interests in responding to ordinary inquiries, steps toward a contract where applicable, or legal obligations where relevant. Correspondence is retained while needed to resolve the inquiry, follow up on related issues, or meet applicable obligations, and should be deleted when those purposes no longer apply.

9. Recipients and international processing

Relevant recipients include the hosting provider, Google when you opt in to Analytics, and the email services used for communications. We do not sell your practice history. Public site content and privacy-choice storage do not require registration.

Providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Depending on the provider, receiving entity, and applicable arrangements, safeguards may include an applicable adequacy framework or standard contractual clauses. Their privacy notices explain the arrangements they use. You may contact us for details applicable to our processing.

10. Your data-protection rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict processing, receive portable data, object to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent. These rights are subject to their legal conditions. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing based on that consent.

Contact contact@typingspeedhub.com. We may need proportionate information to verify a request. Practice data held only in your browser cannot be accessed or deleted remotely by us; use that browser's site-data controls or the relevant tool's reset function.

You may lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority. In Hungary, this is the Hungarian National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH). You may also contact the authority competent for your residence, workplace, or the alleged infringement.

11. Children and safe use

The site includes educational material for younger learners. We do not require children to provide a name, email address, or account to practice. Analytics is not loaded on the dedicated Typing for Kids page. Other pages may offer optional Analytics, so parents or guardians should supervise privacy choices where appropriate.

If you believe a child has sent us personal information that should not have been collected, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. The site does not use an age-verification service.

12. Security and policy changes

The website uses HTTPS. Browser storage can be read by people or software with access to the same browser profile, so avoid using a shared device for sensitive information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

We update this policy when the site's relevant processing changes. The date below identifies the current revision. Check your privacy settings after changing browsers or clearing site data.
