How This Typing Practice Works

Key takeaway: Choose a 1, 5 or 10 minute session, start typing, and focus on maintaining both speed and accuracy. Your personal statistics are saved only in your browser using localStorage.

This practice tool uses the standard typing convention of five characters per word. Your WPM is calculated from correctly typed characters, while accuracy compares correct keystrokes with your total typed characters.

Which Practice Time Should You Choose?

1 minute: useful for a quick daily speed check. 5 minutes: a balanced practice session for speed and consistency. 10 minutes: better for endurance and maintaining accuracy over a longer period.

How WPM Is Calculated

WPM = correct characters ÷ 5 ÷ elapsed minutes

Tips for Better Practice

Keep your eyes on the practice text, use a relaxed hand position, avoid chasing speed at the expense of accuracy, and repeat shorter sessions regularly instead of forcing very long sessions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. Your practice statistics are stored locally in your browser.

Will my results be saved after I close the page?

Yes, as long as your browser keeps localStorage data for this page. Clearing browser site data will remove the saved statistics.

Which duration is best for daily practice?

Five minutes is a practical default for many users because it is long enough to measure consistency while remaining easy to repeat regularly.
