How to practice numbers and symbols

Keep your hands close to the home row and reach upward to the number row without looking down. After each key press, return your finger to its normal position.

For symbols that require Shift, use the Shift key with the opposite hand whenever practical. For example, type a left-side symbol with the right Shift key. This helps maintain balance and touch-typing technique.

The final lessons use practical combinations such as 25%, $19.99, 2026-09-01, 1:30, and 5+5=10. Accuracy matters more than speed, especially when learning symbols.
