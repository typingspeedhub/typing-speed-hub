About Typing Speed Hub

Typing Speed Hub is an independent educational website created to help people improve their typing speed, accuracy, consistency, and confidence. The site provides free typing tests, calculators, practice tools, statistics, drills, and practical guides for users at different skill levels.

The website is designed for students, office workers, remote workers, beginners, and anyone who regularly uses a keyboard and wants to type more efficiently. The goal is to make typing improvement simple, understandable, and accessible without requiring an account or paid subscription.

Who Is Behind Typing Speed Hub?

Tibor Győry is the creator of Typing Speed Hub. He develops and maintains the website and works on its typing practice resources, calculators, tools, and educational guides.

The purpose of the project is practical: to bring typing practice, measurement tools, and clear explanations together in one place. The site is continually developed as new practice features and tools are added and existing pages are reviewed or improved.

Typing Speed Hub does not present Tibor as a professional typing instructor, certified typing examiner, or keyboard hardware tester. Where an article discusses general typing practice or equipment, the goal is to provide useful educational guidance without claiming qualifications or hands-on product testing that the site has not documented.

Our Approach

Typing improvement is not only about pressing keys faster. Accuracy, rhythm, technique, posture, regular practice, and the ability to identify recurring mistakes can all affect typing performance.

The content aims to explain these topics in a practical way. Instead of focusing only on a single high WPM score, Typing Speed Hub encourages users to look at accuracy, consistency, repeated errors, and progress across comparable practice sessions.

How Our Content Is Created and Reviewed

Typing Speed Hub articles are written to answer a specific typing question or help with a specific practice problem. Related topics are separated where possible so that an accuracy guide, an error guide, a structured practice plan, and a typing-speed diagnostic guide each have a clear purpose.

Tools and calculators are designed to give visitors a practical way to measure or work with concepts discussed in the guides. Internal links connect explanations with relevant tests, calculators, trackers, and practice pages when those resources can help with the next step.

Existing pages may be reviewed and updated when a tool changes, an explanation can be made clearer, links need correction, or two topics have become too similar. Updated articles display their revision date where appropriate.

We aim to provide original explanations and useful resources rather than simply repeating general information found elsewhere. Individual typing results can vary depending on experience, keyboard type, language, test length, text difficulty, and practice conditions.

Independence and Transparency
