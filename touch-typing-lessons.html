How the course works

Each lesson keeps all previously learned keys and adds the next keys based on keyboard position and finger movement. New keys are highlighted above the exercise.

Early lessons use short letter groups. As soon as enough keys are available, the exercise automatically begins mixing in real English words. Later lessons use mostly real words while still reinforcing the newest keys. The final lesson adds only the common sentence-ending keys . and ?.

Keep your eyes on the screen. Return your fingers to the home row after each reach, and prioritize clean typing over speed.
