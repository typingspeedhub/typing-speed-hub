Who is this guide for?

It is a starting point for beginners learning to type more confidently, office workers who spend time writing emails and documents, students, and anyone whose speed has stopped improving.

You do not need an impressive starting score. If you already type quickly, use the error-review and plateau sections to make your performance more consistent. The focus is useful typing: comfortable technique and repeatable speed with good accuracy.

The 7-day plan is a practice framework, not a promise of a specific WPM increase. Your progress depends on your starting point, consistency, text difficulty, and technique.

How to use your free typing guide

  1. Measure your starting point. Take a typing speed test and write down your WPM and accuracy. Use similar text difficulty and test duration when you retest.
  2. Read Chapters 1–4. Choose one priority: technique, repeated mistakes, accuracy, or a realistic goal.
  3. Follow the daily routine and 7-day plan. Use touch typing lessons for key placement and timed practice to apply what you learn.
  4. Record meaningful sessions. Print the final tracker page or use the online Typing Progress Tracker. You do not have to fill every day.
  5. Review and adjust. After a week, compare similar tests. If accuracy drops when you speed up, reduce the pace and practice the patterns causing errors.

Questions about the free PDF

Is the guide really free?

Yes. This free edition can be downloaded directly from this page without payment, registration, or an email address.

Can I read it on my phone?

Yes. Open the PDF in your browser or a PDF reader. Because the pages are designed for A4 printing, you may find it easier to zoom in or read on a larger screen.

Which page should I print?

The printable 30-day tracker is on the final page. Select that page in your print settings and use A4 paper. You can also print the full guide if you prefer reading on paper.

What if the PDF opens instead of downloading?

Some browsers open PDF files in their built-in viewer. Use the viewer’s download or save button to keep a copy on your device.

Should I focus on WPM or accuracy first?

Start at a pace where you can type with control. Record both numbers and gradually increase speed while keeping accuracy stable. The guide explains how to use mistakes as practice feedback.

Start with one small improvement

You do not have to change every typing habit at once. Download the guide, take a baseline test, and choose one thing to improve during your next 10-minute session.
